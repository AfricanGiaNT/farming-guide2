# Mlangizi wa Ulimi: Your AI-Powered Farming Partner

In the heart of Malawi, where farming is not just a profession but a way of life, Mlangizi wa Ulimi stands as a beacon of hope for smallholder farmers. Our name, meaning "Farming Guide" in Chichewa, reflects our deep commitment to our roots and our mission.

## What We Do

Imagine having a knowledgeable farming companion in your pocket – one that combines ancient farming wisdom with modern technology. That's Mlangizi wa Ulimi. We've created a free, AI-powered platform that helps farmers make better decisions about their crops, right from their mobile phones.

## Why We Matter

Every year, Malawian farmers lose 20-30% of their precious maize harvest due to poor timing and inadequate information. That's not just numbers – it's families going hungry, children missing meals, and dreams of better harvests slipping away. We're here to change that story.

## Our Promise

We bring together:
- Real-time weather insights that tell you exactly when to plant and harvest
- AI-powered advice that speaks your language and understands your challenges
- Local agricultural knowledge that respects our Malawian farming traditions
- A completely free platform that puts farmers first

## Our Vision

We dream of a Malawi where every farmer has access to the knowledge they need, right when they need it. Where technology serves not as a luxury, but as a bridge to better harvests and fuller tables.

By 2027, we aim to help 750+ farmers increase their yields by 5-10%, feeding an additional 785 people through improved harvests. It's not just about growing more food – it's about growing hope, one farmer at a time.
