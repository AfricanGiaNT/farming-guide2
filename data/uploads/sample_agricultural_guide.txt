Malawi Agricultural Extension Guide
Crop Production and Management

1. MAIZE CULTIVATION

Maize is the staple crop in Malawi and requires careful management for optimal yields.

Planting:
- Plant between November and December for the main season
- Use certified seeds for better germination rates
- Plant at 75cm between rows and 25cm between plants
- Plant 2-3 seeds per hole and thin to 1 plant after germination

Soil Preparation:
- Prepare land 2-3 weeks before planting
- Use proper tillage to break soil compaction
- Apply organic matter or compost to improve soil fertility
- Ensure good drainage to prevent waterlogging

Fertilizer Application:
- Apply NPK fertilizer at planting (basal dressing)
- Apply top dressing 6 weeks after planting
- Use 200kg of 23:21:0+4S per hectare
- Apply fertilizer in bands 5cm from the plant

Pest Management:
- Monitor for stem borer and armyworm
- Use integrated pest management approaches
- Apply appropriate pesticides when thresholds are exceeded
- Practice crop rotation to break pest cycles

2. GROUNDNUT PRODUCTION

Groundnuts are an important cash crop and source of protein.

Varieties:
- CG7: High yielding, drought tolerant
- Khanpur: Early maturing, good for short seasons
- JL24: Disease resistant, suitable for all regions

Planting:
- Plant in December-January
- Use 40kg seed per hectare
- Plant 2 seeds per hole at 30cm spacing
- Ensure good soil contact for proper germination

Harvesting:
- Harvest when pods are mature (90-120 days)
- Lift plants carefully to avoid pod loss
- Dry pods in the sun for 2-3 days
- Store in cool, dry conditions

3. SOIL CONSERVATION

Conservation agriculture practices help maintain soil fertility and prevent erosion.

Key Practices:
- Minimum tillage to preserve soil structure
- Crop rotation with legumes
- Use of cover crops
- Contour farming on slopes
- Mulching to retain moisture

4. WATER MANAGEMENT

Efficient water use is crucial for sustainable agriculture.

Techniques:
- Rainwater harvesting
- Drip irrigation for high-value crops
- Mulching to reduce evaporation
- Proper drainage systems
- Water storage structures

This guide provides essential information for smallholder farmers in Malawi to improve their agricultural productivity and sustainability.
